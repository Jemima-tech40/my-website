Frequently Asked Questions
Common questions about billing and subscriptions
Can I cancel my subscription at any time? +
Yes. You can cancel anytime from your dashboard. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period with no extra charges.
How does the free plan work? +
The free Starter plan is completely free forever. You get 2 service listings and 5 enquiries per month. No credit card required to sign up.
Is my payment information secure? +
Yes. All payments are processed through secure, encrypted channels. We never store your card details on our servers.
Can I upgrade from Basic to Pro? +
Absolutely. You can upgrade anytime. You'll only be charged the difference for the remaining days in your billing cycle.
